This directory holds material that has been retired from the live tree but
kept for reference. Nothing here is gated by CI, and nothing here is deleted.

== proofs/ — retired provers (2026-10-10)

Owner ruling, 2026-10-10: the *active* proof stack is **Idris 2** (the ABI
package) and **Agda** (CNO, OND and EchoBridge). The following provers were
moved here with `git mv`, so their history is preserved:

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Archived path | What it was | Status
| `archive/proofs/coq/` | Coq theories: CNO and OND pillars, 14 theories, `coq_makefile` build, Print Assumptions and axiom-tag gates | Not built or gated. Kept for reference.
| `archive/proofs/lean4/` | Lean 4 CNO and OND libraries, `lake` build, `check-core.sh` axiom audit | Not built or gated.
| `archive/proofs/isabelle/` | Isabelle/HOL session `AbsoluteZero-CNO` and `OND.thy` | Not built or gated.

| `archive/proofs/verify-all-provers.six-prover.sh` | The six-prover gate (Coq, Agda, Lean, Z3, Isabelle, Mizar, Idris 2) | Superseded by `proofs/verify-active-provers.sh`.
| `archive/proofs/tests/gate-selftest.six-prover.sh` | Self-test for the six-prover gate (stubbed toolchains, z3 mutants) | Superseded. Kept for reference only.
|===

=== Why

* Owner ruling, 2026-10-10: use Idris 2 and Agda as the active proof stack, and
  file the other provers in this archive. The owner's rationale is not restated
  here; add it if it should be on record.
* Archiving is reversible. A prover returns to the active tree by moving its
  directory back and restoring its gate.

=== What was NOT archived

* `proofs/agda/` (active; CNO, OND, EchoBridge)
* `proofs/z3/` (active as a *solver* of bounded, expect-annotated checks, not as a
  proof assistant; see the flag below)
* `proofs/observation-models/` and `proofs/residue/` (not prover trees)
* `src/abi/` and `absolute-zero-abi.ipkg` (Idris 2 ABI)

== Flags for the owner (open)

. *Coq and D204.* D204 (2026-09-30) defines the CNO interface as a Coq Module
  Type that `valence-shell` imports. Archiving Coq conflicts with that decision.
  The ruling needed is whether the Module Type moves to Agda, or whether Coq is
  restored for that interface alone.
. *Agda and LANGUAGE-POLICY.* `CLAUDE.md` allows Agda. The language policy names
  Idris 2 as the sole formal-verification option. The active stack uses both, so
  the policy needs a standards ruling.
. *Z3.* Kept active as a solver of bounded checks, not as a proof assistant. Flagged
  so the choice is visible.
. *TIERS.* TIERS names Coq and Isabelle at the Rhodium tier. Neither is in the
  allowed-provers table. Unresolved.
