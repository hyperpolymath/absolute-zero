= absolute-zero — Proof Status
:toc:

[abstract]
absolute-zero formalises *two co-equal pillars* (see `docs/TWO-PILLARS.adoc`):
*CNO* — Certified Null Operations (certified null *effect*) — and *OND* —
Observational Null Disclosure (certified null *disclosure*). This document
records the *actually reproduced* verification state in this environment, prover
by prover, and is explicit about which axioms remain and why.

=== Design audit (2026-11-09)

A statement-level review of the active Agda (`proofs/agda/` at `cbe9ae9`)
against the claims in this file, README, EXPLAINME and `docs/TWO-PILLARS.adoc`.
The terms all type-check: on 2026-11-09 the four modules at `cbe9ae9` were rechecked cold
with Agda 2.6.3 and agda-stdlib 2.3 (CI pairs Agda 2.6.3 with agda-stdlib 1.7.3) under
`agda --safe --without-K --cubical-compatible`, and all passed. That is a local check, not a CI
receipt. The findings concern what the terms *state*.

[cols="2,3,2",options="header"]
|===
| Item | What the Agda actually states | Honest label

| `IsCNO` (`CNO.agda`)
| `state-eq` includes `state-pc`. `step` advances the PC on `Nop`, `Load`,
`Store` and `Add` and sets it on `Jump`; `eval` never restores it. So
`cno-identity : ∀ s → state-eq (eval p s) s` holds only for programs made
of `Halt` instructions (and the empty program). The archived Coq fixed
the same defect on 2026-05-18 by excluding the PC from `state_eq`; the
active Agda did not.
| `IsCNO` is a degenerate class in the active stack. `halt-is-cno`,
`cno-composition` and every result quantified over `IsCNO` are `proven`
but about that class only. *Resolved 2026-11-09:* `IsCNO` now judges a run by `obs-eq` (memory, registers, I/O; no PC). `nop-is-cno` and `jump-exit-is-cno` show the class is not Halt-only; `load-not-cno` shows it is not everything. `state-eq` keeps the PC and is still the full equality.

| `terminates`, `terminates-always`
| `terminates p s = ∃ s′. eval p s ≡ s′`, inhabited by `(eval p s , refl)`
for every program. `eval` is a total fold over the instruction list.
| Holds by construction; *not* a termination result. *Resolved 2026-11-09:* `eval` is replaced by a pc-driven machine: a big-step relation `_⊢_⇓_` and a fuel interpreter `run`, related by `run-sound` and `run-complete`. `Terminates p s` asks for a normal stop from pc 0, and `jump-self-diverges` shows `Jump 0` never stops. A Store or Add on a missing register faults and does not terminate.

| `thermodynamically-reversible`, `energy-dissipated`
| `energy-dissipated _ _ _ = zero`, so every program, reversible or not,
satisfies it. `reversible` is defined but never used.
| Holds by construction; *not* a Landauer/Bennett result. The archived Coq
`StatMech`/`LandauerDerivation` development is the only thermodynamic
formalisation, and it is archived. *Resolved 2026-11-09:* `energy-dissipated`, `thermodynamically-reversible` and the unused `reversible` are removed. `cno-left-inverse` proves observational reversibility (running any CNO after a CNO restores memory, registers and I/O) as a consequence of the identity law, and claims nothing thermodynamic.

| OND-5 "mirrored in Lean 4, Agda, Z3"
| Agda states OND-5 under `O-out`; the archived Coq and Lean state it under
`O_all`. Both are true; they are different propositions.
| Correspondence state `unassessed` (PROOF-POLICY §2.2). The rows are not
mirrors of one theorem.

| OND-3 "anchored to `is_CNO`"
| `OND.agda` uses a local `is-null` and never imports `CNO`. The anchoring
(`writer_program_not_core_CNO`, `skip_program_is_core_CNO`) exists only in
the archived Coq.
| The active Agda OND-3 is *not* anchored to `IsCNO`.

| EchoBridge
| The `≡`-form results (`echo-from-cno-program-≡`, `absolute-zero-echo-≡`)
take funext as a hypothesis; the `EchoRel` results do not. The echo is the
fixed-point witness `(s , cno-identity)`.
| `proven`, conditional on funext where stated; inherits the `IsCNO`
degeneracy above. *Resolved 2026-11-09:* restated over `cno-result` and `obs-eq`; the `≡` form is now on the observable projection `observe`, and `program-state-model` is a `CNOModel` over observable states.
|===

=== Role of this repository (consumer, not estate-wide ground truth)

absolute-zero is a *consumer and domain formalisation*: it applies standard
results (Landauer 1961, Bennett 1973, the non-interference literature,
category theory) to its own CNO/OND domain concepts. Its historical prover
counts (above) are evidence about its own archived trees only, and must not
be cited as proof status for any other repository.

*Cross-repo note (2026-11-01).* `epistemic-types` (at `5422cd5`) is an

cross-repo claim must therefore name the exact theorem, its scope, and its
assumptions (e.g. "OND-1..5, `proven`, `ungraded`, Agda `--safe --without-K`,
relative to a declared `O`") — never a bare count imported from this repo.
Mathesis (the estate mathematical knowledge commons) may *catalogue* these
receipts and grades, but per its own architecture the original repositories
remain the sovereign owners of their mathematics; this file is the local
proof-status record of record.

=== Reversibility <-> CNO bridge (2026-07-16, the theorem MAA cites)

`archive/proofs/coq/common/CNO.v` now carries the bridge between `reversible` and the
"composes to a no-op" characterisation the MAA framework (aletheia) cites. All five
new results are **`Closed under the global context` (zero axioms)**:

* `reversible_iff_exists_reverses` — the repo's `reversible` is exactly "exists a
  one-sided left inverse" (`exists p_inv, reverses p p_inv`).
* `reverses_seq_computes_identity` — the core lemma: a left inverse sequenced after
  `p` computes the state-identity on every composite transition (via `eval_app` +
  `eval_deterministic`).
* `cno_equiv_seq_empty_of_reverses` — repackaged as `cno_equiv (p ;; p_inv) []`
  (CNO-equivalence to the canonical no-op).
* `reversible_bridge_forward` — the faithful *forward* direction of the aletheia
  contract: a **two-sided** inverse makes *both* composites CNO-equivalent to the
  no-op.
* `reversible_bridge_backward_upto` — the *backward* direction, **necessarily up to
  `=st=`**, with an explicit `p_inv`-termination hypothesis.

*Honesty note.* The literal biconditional
`reversible p <-> exists p_inv, is_CNO (p;;p_inv) /\ is_CNO (p_inv;;p)` is **not**
provable under these definitions, and is deliberately *not* asserted: (1) `is_CNO`
also demands purity and totality, which a `reversible` program need not have — so
"=== CNO" is rendered by `cno_equiv _ []` (the identity-on-state component); (2)
`state_eq` excludes the program counter while `eval` propagates it (the
`eval_respects_state_eq_*` axioms were removed as unsound on 2026-05-20), forcing the
backward direction to be up-to-`=st=`; (3) the repo's `reversible` is one-sided
whereas the contract is two-sided. The in-file section header documents all three.
This is a genuine statement-shape finding: it tells the aletheia contract to be
stated two-sided and `=st=`-relative, not a proof dodged.
